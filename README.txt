FIRST FLOOR — SMITH HALL v0.10: LIGHTING & SOUND

Installation:
1. Back up your working v0.9 project.
2. Copy main.py, building.py, the textures/ folder, and the sounds/ folder
   into your existing PyCharm project, preserving the folder structure.
3. Install Ursina if needed: pip install ursina
4. Run main.py.

New in v0.10:
- Warm fluorescent fixture glow geometry and gentle ambient lighting.
- Low-volume continuous fluorescent hum and elevator machinery hum.
- Carpet footsteps while walking or running.
- Sliding-door sound effects and ending chime.
- M toggles game audio. The other controls and v0.8 story branches remain.

The sound effects are original procedurally generated WAV files. No
external audio files or downloads are required. Volume may depend on
your system and audio device. Ursina audio and lighting behavior have
not been runtime-tested here; please test in PyCharm.
